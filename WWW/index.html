Setup

Emergency contact

Saved only on this device. During SOS you get one-tap Message and Call buttons.

External push buttons (ESP32)

  • GPIO 27 Camera on/off
  • GPIO 13 Voice on/off
  • GPIO 14 Mic on/off
  • GPIO 25 SOS (press again to cancel)

Other leg of each button goes to GND. Connect the ESP32 first. Keyboard keys also work: 1-4 or C, V, M, S.

Ultrasonic sensor (HC-SR04)

  • VCC to ESP32 5V (VIN)
  • GND to GND
  • TRIG to GPIO 5
  • ECHO to GPIO 18 through a voltage divider (1k + 2k) because ECHO is 5V

Flash firmware/esp32_rose/esp32_rose.ino, then connect the ESP32. Distance shows on the Detect tab and objects are announced with their distance. Say “how far”. Warning beeps start closer than 30 cm.

Voice commands

  • “camera on” / “camera off”
  • “voice on” / “voice off”, “mic off”
  • “who is this”, “what do you see”, “how far”
  • “save face as name”, “delete name”, “list faces”
  • “find bottle”, “where is my phone”, “stop finding”
  • “SOS” or “emergency”, “cancel SOS”
  • “go to faces / setup / detect”, “commands”

Tap Mic once to start listening; after that everything works hands-free. Needs Chrome.

About this app

The camera recognises objects and speaks their names. Saved faces are announced by name and can be sent to an ESP32. Requires HTTPS and Chrome.
